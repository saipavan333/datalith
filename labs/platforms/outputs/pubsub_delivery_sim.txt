What to look for: a slow ack makes Pub/Sub redeliver; the naive sink double-counts revenue,
the idempotent sink keyed on message_id (or a business key) lands exactly the right total.
Note: here a slow message is simply retried until one attempt acks in time. In production you
extend the deadline (modifyAckDeadline / client lease management) or set a dead-letter topic.
