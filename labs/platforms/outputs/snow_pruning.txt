same answer both ways: 7658163.95 == 7658163.95 -> True

What to look for:
 - Random arrival order puts every date in almost every partition: nothing can be pruned.
 - Sorted (clustered) data gives each partition a narrow date range, so ~97% are skipped.
 - 'depth' is SYSTEM$CLUSTERING_DEPTH's idea: how many partitions overlap one value. Lower is better.
